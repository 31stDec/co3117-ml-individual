My cheat sheet helped with about half of the parts I was able to answer. It was useful, but I think I could have performed better if I had spent more time reviewing the theory and kept the sheet focused on essential formulas. For future exams, I want to understand the material first and use the cheat sheet mainly as a quick reminder.

After the midterm, I plan to revisit the GA theory and practise more questions, especially the roles of fitness, selection, crossover, and mutation. My main goal is to build a stronger understanding and rely less on looking up information during the exam.
